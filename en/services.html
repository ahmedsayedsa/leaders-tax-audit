Our Services

Eight integrated services delivered by one team from our firm — choose what fits your company's current stage, or let us review your file and propose the right scope.

Audit & Assurance

Certified financial statements are a basic requirement for dealing with banks, investors and regulators. We audit your statements under Egyptian and international accounting and auditing standards, and issue an independent professional opinion that reflects your company's true financial position.

  • Annual financial statement audits and issuance of the auditor's report
  • Limited reviews of interim financial statements
  • Agreed-upon procedures on specific items
  • Audits for special purposes: bank financing, partner entry, capital increases

Tax & Tax Inspections

Tax obligations in Egypt are numerous and their deadlines are close together, and mistakes are costly. We manage your entire tax file: correct returns on time, and strong technical representation during inspections that protects your position and reduces differences.

  • Income tax, VAT, payroll, stamp duty and withholding returns
  • Attending tax inspections and representation before tax offices
  • Appeal memoranda and representation before internal and appeal committees
  • Lawful tax planning that reduces your burden within the law

Bookkeeping & Monthly Accounting

Orderly books from day one mean faster decisions and a calmer tax inspection. We run the full accounting cycle and send you clear monthly reports on a fixed date.

  • Journal entries and classification on a chart of accounts designed for your activity
  • Bank reconciliations and tracking of receivables and payables
  • Fixed asset, depreciation and inventory records
  • Monthly and annual financial statements and reports

Company Formation

Choosing the wrong legal form is paid for over years. We accompany you from comparing the alternatives until the activity actually starts, and stay with you for the recurring obligations after formation.

  • Choosing the legal form best suited to your activity and number of partners
  • Preparing the articles of association and bylaws and completing the documents
  • Commercial register, tax card and VAT registration when needed
  • Amendments to articles, general assemblies and liquidation when required

E-Invoicing & E-Receipts

Joining the Tax Authority's platform is mandatory and its phases are sequential; delay exposes you to fines and disrupts your dealings with large companies. We prepare you fully and link your system without disrupting your work.

  • Registration on the platform and obtaining the electronic signature
  • Item and service coding (GS1 / EGS)
  • Linking your accounting software or ERP to the platform
  • Training your team and monitoring issuance during the first period

Feasibility Studies & Valuation

An investment or bank financing decision starts from a study built on real numbers, not impressions. We prepare economic feasibility studies and fair-value valuations using methodologies recognised locally and internationally.

  • Feasibility studies for new projects and expansions
  • Company valuations for mergers, acquisitions and partner entry
  • Studies supporting bank financing applications
  • Financial analysis and performance indicators for management

Internal Audit & Governance

The bigger your company grows, the greater the need for control that does not depend on individuals. We build you an internal control system and document cycles that protect assets and clarify responsibilities.

  • Risk-based internal audit plans
  • Design of document cycles and approval policies
  • Governance regulations and policies sized to your company
  • Assessment and development of existing internal control

Payroll & Social Insurance

Payroll is a monthly item that tolerates no error: employment income tax, insurance and strict statutory deadlines. We handle it accurately and with complete confidentiality every month.

  • Payroll preparation and calculation of deductions
  • Quarterly and annual employment income tax returns
  • Social insurance forms and deadline tracking
  • End-of-service settlements and entitlements

Not sure which service you need?

Tell us the situation, not the service: a bank requesting certified statements, an approaching tax deadline, overdue books, or a new company — and we will point you to the right scope.

About Us

An accounting, audit and tax firm built on a simple idea: that a business owner should know his financial and tax position clearly, at any time, from one team he trusts.

Our Story

Why "Leaders"?

Leaders for Taxation and Auditing — Momen Abdelazeem & Co was founded in response to a recurring problem at Egyptian companies: books kept at the last moment, returns prepared under deadline pressure, and tax inspections that surprise the business owner with differences he was not prepared for.

We work differently: continuous follow-up throughout the year, not only when obligations fall due. Your file is reviewed periodically, your statutory deadlines are calculated in advance, and any legislative change affecting your activity reaches you explained before it turns into a missed obligation.

We serve Egyptian companies of all sizes, and foreign investors wishing to establish their businesses in Egypt, with reports in Arabic and English when needed.

Our Values

Principles that do not change with the client

Independence

Our professional opinion is not influenced by any interest — we tell you the truth about your numbers as they are, because a flattering opinion today means a bigger problem tomorrow.

Accuracy

We build our work on verification and documentation, not assumption. Every figure in your statements has a document supporting it and a review confirming it.

Confidentiality

We safeguard the information our clients entrust to us in all circumstances; only the direct working team accesses your file.

Deadline Commitment

Statutory deadlines for returns, inspections and renewals are a red line — managed with an advance calendar and alerts before they fall due.

Clarity

We explain your position and the required steps in direct language without excess professional complexity — you always know where you stand and what comes next.

Continuity

We accompany our clients throughout the year, not only when obligations fall due — a long-term professional partnership, not a seasonal transaction.

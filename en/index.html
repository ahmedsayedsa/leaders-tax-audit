FAQ

Short answers to the questions we hear most

Registration is mandatory for anyone selling a taxable good or service once total sales over twelve months reach the statutory registration threshold of EGP 500,000, and in some cases registration is required regardless of that threshold. We determine your exact position after reviewing your activity.
A system run by the Egyptian Tax Authority for issuing and receiving invoices electronically between businesses with real-time validation; its counterpart for final consumers is the e-receipt. We handle registration, item coding and linking your accounting system to the platform.
The tax office examines your books and returns for specific years, and the inspection may result in tax differences. We represent you before the tax office and appeal committees, and prepare the documents and technical defences that protect your position and reduce differences to the lowest lawful level.
The timeline depends on the legal form, the activity, required approvals and document readiness. We manage the process from choosing the legal form through the commercial register and tax card, and give you a realistic timetable before we start.
Fees depend on the scope of work, the size of the activity and the number of transactions, and are documented in a clear agreement before work begins — with no surprises later. Send us your details and you will receive the scope and fees.

Tell us what you are facing — a bank requesting certified statements, an approaching tax deadline, an ongoing inspection, or a new company to form — and we will reply with a clear scope of work and defined fees.
